Privacy

Last updated September 26, 2026

What we collect

  • Email list. Your email address, if you join the list.
  • Order requests. Your name, email, shipping address, any notes, and the pieces in your cart, if you send an order request.

Why

To tell you when a run opens, and to confirm, make and ship your order. We use it for nothing else.

Who receives it

Form submissions are received through the form service that hosts this site, and are used by Anthos Atelier to reply to you and fulfil orders. Payment, when you approve an order, is handled by a separate payment provider through a link we email you; we don’t collect card details on this site.

On your device

This site stores two small things in your browser: your cart, so it survives a reload, and a note that you confirmed you are 21 or older. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers on this page.

Your choices

Write to us and we will remove you from the list or delete your order details. You can clear the items stored in your browser at any time from your browser settings.

Age

This site is for adults aged 21 and over.
