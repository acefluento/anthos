Questions

How long does an order take?

Every piece is made to order in limited runs, and orders ship in 5–7 days. Shipping is complimentary over $150.

How does ordering work?

Add pieces to your cart and send us your order request. We confirm your pieces and email you a secure payment link. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

What is your returns policy?

Unused pieces can be returned within 30 days.

What does “blind deboss” mean?

The wordmark is pressed into the surface with no ink and no foil. The depth is held at 0.35 mm across glass, ceramic and metal, so you read it in the shadow rather than in flat light.

Do you sell cannabis?

No. Anthos Atelier makes accessories only. We don’t sell the plant.

Who can order?

You need to be 21 or older to enter this site and to place an order.

Runs are small and they go quietly.
